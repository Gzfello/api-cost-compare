FAQ

What is a token?

1 token = approximately 0.75 words = 4 characters in English. A 1,000-word article = approximately 1,300 tokens.

Are these prices guaranteed?

No. Prices change frequently. Check provider pricing pages for current rates. Many offer free tiers.

Which is cheapest overall?

For raw cost: Groq. For capability per dollar: DeepSeek. For features: OpenAI or Anthropic.
