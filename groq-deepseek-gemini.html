Deep dive: Groq

Pros:

  • Cheapest per token by far
  • Fastest inference (654 tokens/sec on Llama 3.1)
  • Simple API, OpenAI-compatible
  • Free tier available

Cons:

  • Llama 3.1 8B: limited reasoning
  • No multimodal input
  • Smaller context window
  • Less ecosystem tooling

Deep dive: DeepSeek

Pros:

  • Best capability per dollar
  • Strong reasoning (DeepSeek R1)
  • Open weights
  • Large context windows

Cons:

  • Chinese provider (privacy considerations)
  • Slower than Groq/Gemini
  • Limited free tier
  • Access can be inconsistent

Deep dive: Gemini

Pros:

  • Great balance of price + quality
  • Multimodal (text, image, audio)
  • Google ecosystem integration
  • Generous free tier

Cons:

  • Rate limits on free tier
  • Prices going up in 2027
  • Privacy: Google data collection
